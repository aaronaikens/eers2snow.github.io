What I do

At Novus Labs, I lead cross-functional teams doing hardware validation and wireless testing for consumer electronics brands you've heard of — Google, Nintendo, Bose, Xbox, and a lot of others. If it needs to be proven before it ships, that's us.

Fifteen-plus years in test & validation. Teams up to 40 engineers. I'm also deep into building AI tooling into how engineering organizations actually work — not just talking about it.

A good day at work is when testing finds something unexpected. Not because I enjoy delivering bad news — but because that's exactly the moment a test program shows its value.
